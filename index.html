CORE CAPABILITIES

Protection at Every Layer

01

Real-Time Detection

Advanced sensors and algorithms monitor line parameters continuously, detecting breaks within milliseconds of occurrence.

02

Automatic Isolation

Upon detection, Smart LT instantly isolates the affected section, preventing cascading failures and protecting connected equipment.

03

Predictive Analytics

Machine learning models analyze historical data to predict potential line failures before they occur, enabling proactive maintenance.

04

Multi-Zone Architecture

Hierarchical protection zones ensure granular control and monitoring across the entire transmission network.

05

Intelligent Restoration

After fault clearance, the system automatically tests and restores power to unaffected sections, minimizing downtime.

06

Compliance Engine

Built-in compliance monitoring ensures all operations meet IEC 61850, IEEE C37, and regional safety standards.

HOW IT WORKS

From Detection to Protection

01

High-Frequency Sampling

Edge Units continuously sample voltage, current, and impedance at 10kHz+

02

Waveform ML Analysis

On-device ML models analyze waveforms for break signatures in < 50ms

03

Fault Correlation

Zone Controllers correlate data from multiple EUs to confirm and locate the fault

04

Automated Trip Signals

Automatic trip signals isolate the affected section via IEC 61850 GOOSE messaging

05

Autonomous Restoration

System tests adjacent sections and restores power to unaffected zones

06

Continuous Optimization

All events are logged, analyzed, and fed back into predictive models
